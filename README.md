## What is FINDit?

FINDit is a simple, privacy-first public directory of:

- Adult retail stores (online & physical)
- Shops (lingerie, fetish, lifestyle)
- Events (parties, workshops, expos)
- Sellers (new & second-hand)
- Manufacturers & branding establishments
- Service providers within BDSM and adult communities

It is **not** affiliated with any listed business. We only list publicly operating entities. Users deal directly with the businesses.

## Features

- Age gate (18+)

## South African Legal Context (summary)

> This is **not legal advice**. Laws change. Always verify with a qualified professional or official sources.

### Key laws relevant to adult content & communities

| Law / Framework | Relevance |
|-----------------|-----------|
| **Films and Publications Act 65 of 1996** (as amended) | Classification of films, publications and games. Adult premises and distribution rules. Private sexual images (revenge porn) prohibited. |
| **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)** | Governs collection, storage and use of personal data. FINDit collects only what is needed for the contact form. |
| **Sexual Offences Act 23 of 1957** + **Criminal Law (Sexual Offences and Related Matters) Amendment Act** | Currently criminalises the buying and selling of sexual services between adults. Decriminalisation legislation is in advanced stages (2025–2026) but **not yet law**. |
| **Prevention and Combating of Trafficking in Persons Act 7 of 2013** | Trafficking remains a serious crime. Consenting adult activity is distinct from trafficking. |
| **Children’s Act** | Strict protections for minors. FINDit is 18+ only. |

**Important notes (as of 2026):**
- Adult sex work remains criminalised under existing legislation, although a decriminalisation bill is being finalised and a prosecution moratorium has been discussed in some contexts.
- Online adult content creation (e.g. platforms similar to OnlyFans) operates in a grey area but income is still taxable under normal tax rules.
- Distributing private sexual images without consent is a criminal offence.
- Always obtain clear, ongoing consent. Respect boundaries, safewords and aftercare in BDSM contexts.

FINDit is a **directory only**. We do not facilitate transactions, bookings or any commercial activity between users and listed parties.
